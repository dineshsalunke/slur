# SLUR — Audio Credits

The sci-fi set (#267, ADR-021) mixes **CC0** sounds with three **CC-BY** sound effects and one **CC-BY-SA**
music track. The CC-BY and CC-BY-SA attributions below are legally required. Every cut was trimmed,
faded, gain-matched, downmixed to mono (SFX) and re-encoded to Ogg Opus; those are the changes the
licences ask us to state.
