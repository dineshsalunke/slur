# SLUR — Asset Acknowledgments

SLUR is built on freely-licensed third-party assets. This file consolidates every
external asset used and its license. Most are **CC0** (public domain, no attribution
required) — we credit them anyway, out of respect for the authors. One track is
**CC-BY 4.0** and its attribution is legally **required** (see below).

Full per-file audio breakdown lives in
[`apps/client/public/audio/CREDITS.md`](apps/client/public/audio/CREDITS.md).

## Sound effects

All SFX are **CC0** from **[Kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/assets)** — packs *Sci-Fi
Sounds*, *Interface Sounds*, *Digital Audio*, and *Impact Sounds*. The local ship's
engine hum is synthesized in-engine (Web Audio, no sample). Per-file mapping:
[`apps/client/public/audio/CREDITS.md`](apps/client/public/audio/CREDITS.md).
