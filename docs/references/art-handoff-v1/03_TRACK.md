# 03 — Track Visual Language

## Core track direction

The track is intentionally minimal:

- dark graphite / black metallic surface
- large clean panel divisions
- sparse fine seams
- restrained gloss / reflection
- functional marigold edge and energy channels
- no ornamental greebling

There are **no slow blocks and no special floor types** in the frozen art direction.

Golden reference: `07_track_visual_language_final.png`

## Track boundaries

Track edges should read clearly at speed through a continuous or near-continuous marigold energy treatment.

The boundary glow is functional: it defines the playable ribbon and helps reinforce perspective and speed.

Avoid making every panel seam equally emissive. The edge is the primary guide.

## Reflection

Use enough reflection to carry warm energy across the dark surface, but do not turn the floor into a mirror.

Desired read:

- dark base
- readable panel break-up
- broad, restrained warm reflection
- clear silhouettes of hazards and ship

## Gaps

Gaps are missing track geometry, not special floor materials.

They may be:

- partial width
- full width
- very small
- large

Production geometry is continuous. Concept-board lane examples are illustrative only.

Golden reference: `08_gap_variations_reference.png`

### Small-gap readability

Tiny gaps can disappear visually because of perspective and track thickness. Use the frozen balanced treatment:

1. **Subtle marigold edge definition** around the opening
2. **Slight inner-lip glow** to reveal the cut
3. **Darker inner cavity** to strengthen depth contrast

Avoid decorative warning brackets, large icons, or unrelated warning colors.

The goal is not to decorate the gap; it is to make the missing geometry legible.

Golden reference: `09_small_gap_readability_final.png`

## Finish line

The finish should feel like a destination without becoming a noisy spectacle.

Use the same track/world language:

- large, clean structural form
- marigold functional energy
- clear silhouette from distance
- controlled reveal as the player approaches

## A → B → C on the track

Do not redesign the track for each intensity state.

Vary:

- glow intensity
- reflection intensity
- atmospheric response
- environmental light spill

Keep geometry/readability stable.
