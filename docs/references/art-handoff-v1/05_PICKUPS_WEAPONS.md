# 05 — Pickups & Weapons

Golden reference: `11_pickups_weapons_final.png`

The family follows one rule:

> **Shared construction language, distinct silhouettes, distinct motion/energy behavior.**

Use dark materials + marigold energy consistently, but make each item recognizable at speed.

## Bolt

- elongated / linear visual behavior
- near-instant projectile read
- active form is an energy streak/tracer, not a sphere
- precise and aggressive

## Boost

- forward-oriented chevron / directional pickup language
- active effect is broad, forward, energetic thrust
- should communicate acceleration immediately

## Shield

- circular / enclosing pickup silhouette
- active state forms a stable protective energy envelope around the ship
- calm, stable visual behavior compared with offensive items

## Mine

- compact radial/star-like pickup form
- dropped hazard must remain clearly visible on/near the track
- persistent, dangerous, area-denial read

## Homing Seeker — FINAL FORM

The chosen seeker is **Variant 1: square-profile, elongated, four fins**.

Do not revert to a conventional tapered missile/rocket silhouette.

Frozen characteristics:

- square cross-section
- longer sleek rectangular body
- no body taper / no middle bulge
- 4 fins, one per side
- bright rear/core energy signature
- large enough active silhouette to be readable in the HUD rear-view mirror
- strong rectangular/architectural form consistent with the wider SLUR language

The concept exploration's octagonal 8-fin variant is **not selected**.

## Pickup vs active state

Every item should have a clear relationship between:

- what the player collects
- what appears when the item is used

The pickup may be abstracted, but it should preview the item's role/silhouette/energy behavior.
