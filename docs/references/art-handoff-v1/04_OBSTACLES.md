# 04 — Obstacle Blocks

Obstacle blocks are immediate gameplay hazards and must read differently from environmental monoliths.

Golden reference: `10_obstacle_blocks_final.png`

## Standard deadly block

Visual language:

- clean
- solid
- monolithic
- simple silhouette
- dark material family shared with track/world
- sparse marigold seams

Read at a glance as:

> **sealed mass — avoid it**

Avoid unnecessary chips, decals, greebles, or decorative damage.

## Destructible block

Use the same base family and proportions so it belongs to the same world, but communicate structural weakness clearly.

Visual cues:

- visible fracture segmentation
- stronger crack pattern than standard blocks
- brighter marigold internal energy visible through fractures
- silhouette remains simple

Read at a glance as:

> **stressed shell — can be broken**

## Destruction feedback

Desired visual sequence:

1. intact but visibly fractured
2. hit / energy builds through cracks
3. break / fragments separate
4. brief marigold energy burst and clear opening

This is an **art behavior**, not a mandate for runtime fracture simulation.

## Environment integration

Obstacle readability is invariant across A/B/C. Environmental intensity may increase, but obstacle silhouettes and contrast must stay clear.
