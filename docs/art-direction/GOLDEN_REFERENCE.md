- Cold desaturated cosmic depth, immense stone scenery, dark engineered track and gameplay objects, golden marigold energy.
- Nebula panel of the original board is the compositional baseline. The asteroid panel is a density/framing variation; other established sector families are not removed.
- Track: dark satin graphite metal, rectangular tiles, subtle surface variation, warm broad reflections. Continuous bright perimeter strips; thin gap lips; sparse **intermittent interior seam lights** with varied lengths and irregular spacing. No fully glowing tile grid, marked driving lanes or automatic safe route.
- Use 4u tiles as a scale/authoring study ruler: 16 across 64u. This is not a new collision quantization rule. The generated image does not reliably depict that exact count.
- Monoliths: immense matte stone/worn concrete, with **visible vertical marigold seams and localized soft halos**, subordinate to gameplay light. Do not remove their glow or make their whole faces metal.
- Obstacles: smooth coated-metal sealed masses versus broken-contour shells with recessed warm energy. All retain an 8u height envelope. The scene does not yet prove adequate silhouette recognition.
- Gaps: real missing floor, thin edge light, visible slab section and open space. User prefers connected missing-tile shapes with stepped outlines and attached surviving floor tongues. Near-edge example retains a supporting outer floor strip and straight boundary. This is art/layout intent, not authorization to change gap simulation rules or global gap lengths.
- Pickups: physical shaded shells and luminous cores; somewhat enlarged for distance recognition. Exact pickup dimensions remain unapproved.
- Active boost includes long energetic exhaust and **subtle environmental motion blur**, mostly nearest track and peripheral scenery. The stronger blur explorations are cinematic studies, not the normal gameplay target. The approved image still exaggerates blur relative to the verbal target.
- Combat illustration includes a second ship and pursuing square-profile, untapered, four-fin Seeker. No new gameplay behavior is established by the illustration.
