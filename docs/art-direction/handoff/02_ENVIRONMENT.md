## Monoliths

### Frozen silhouettes

- **Obelisk**
- **Gate**
- **Arch**

### Material

- dark stone
- worn concrete family
- solid, heavy, matte-to-subtle-gloss read

### Surface language

- clean large panel lines where needed
- subtle or near-zero wear at gameplay distance
- sparse marigold seams only
- may be almost completely solid if that reads better

### Role

Monoliths are **environmental anchors**, not gameplay hazards.

They:

- establish scale
- frame the track
- create rhythm
- help communicate environmental intensity

They must not visually masquerade as deadly gameplay blocks.

## Asteroids

### Frozen form language

Core:

- Angular
- Plate
- Broken

Variations:

- Elongated
- Shattered
- Cluster

**Holed asteroids are removed.**

### Surface language

- dark stone / desaturated rocky surface
- readable large fractures
- rare marigold energy veins
- energy veins are accents, not the default asteroid state

Avoid noisy micro-detail. The silhouette matters more than the surface at speed.

### Role

Asteroids supply organic contrast to the monoliths and provide foreground/midground/background depth.

A→B→C increases:

- density
- size
- proximity
- selective energy-vein frequency

## Planets & moons

### Frozen core types

- Gas giant
- Moon
- Crescent / eclipse body

### Preferred lighting

- Day side
- Backlit
- Eclipse

### Preferred compositions

- Giant + moon
- Distant worlds
- Close horizon

Planets/moons are compositional anchors, not gameplay objects. Keep them cold and desaturated. Do not add marigold surface coloration by default.

## Sector concepts

Frozen sector families:

### 1. Monolith Field

Approved as a clean, sparse, monumental sector.

### 2. Asteroid Gauntlet

Use **controlled/moderate** density. The earlier overly cluttered version was rejected. It should feel dangerous without becoming visual noise.

### 3. Planetary Horizon

Keep the large horizon anchor and add some asteroids higher in the frame to prevent the upper composition from feeling empty.

### 4. Distant Worlds

Use layered worlds/moons and a little more upper-frame filler. Keep fillers higher and away from the immediate gameplay corridor.

### 5. Eclipse Corridor

Approved strongly as-is: dramatic, mysterious, high-contrast celestial framing.

### 6. Convergence

Mixed planet + asteroids + monoliths. Reduce clutter by roughly **10–20%** compared with the first exploration. It should feel climactic, not unreadable.
