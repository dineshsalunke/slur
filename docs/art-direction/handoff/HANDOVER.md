## Final visual contract

- Deep dark graphite and stone, faint desaturated slate-blue surroundings, localized golden marigold. Gameplay has the strongest signals. No saturated blue wash or lifted grey shadows.
- [Golden reference 17](../golden-reference/DIRECTION.md) governs scene integration and the compact borderless HUD.
- [Track](../track/README.md): satin bare graphite, broad warm reflections, continuous outer boundaries, sparse irregular seam inserts, thin gap lips and frozen procedural wear at board 25's shown strength.
- [Background](../scene-background/README.md): planet-led Deep Space; nebula-led Nebula with small subordinate planets.
- [Progression](../progression/README.md): Calm / Balanced / Intense through density, scale, proximity, enclosure and selective prop lighting. Recovery beats are valid.
- [Non-destructible blocks](../desctructible-block/README.md): clean/worn coated metal, sealed silhouettes, variable vertical seams. Wear does not turn them into fractured stone.
- Destructible blocks use broad recessed fractures and broken outer contours with internal marigold energy. Surface cracks alone do not establish the required read. No collision or ramming decision is made here.
- Monoliths retain Obelisk/Gate/Arch forms in matte stone/worn concrete, with visible selective marigold seams and localized halos. Asteroids retain Angular/Plate/Broken forms and Elongated/Shattered/Cluster variations, with rare energy veins. No holed family.
- [Pickup family 11](../boards/11_pickups_weapons_final.png) retains identity: Bolt tracer, Boost chevrons, Shield enclosure, radial Mine, elongated untapered square four-fin Seeker. Pickups have real shaded volume, dark shells and luminous cores.

## Geometry and production

Use the [scale reference](../../ART_SCALE_REFERENCE.md) and current gameplay contracts. Track width is 64u, authoring ruler 4u, obstacle height 8u; widths and depths vary. Continuous simulation is not a tile grid. Ship proportions differ by class; Comet is wider than long.

Gaps are missing floor with dark slab cut edges and open space. Do not fill the void with a cavity material or black floor. Generated gap shapes and apparent slab depths do not change collision rules.

Prefer procedural models and materials, especially for track wear and block variation. Choose techniques by measured cost and visual fidelity; final art does not prescribe a shader API or certify performance. Reflection, illumination and bloom are separate requirements.

Read [production validation](../PRODUCTION_VALIDATION.md) for moving-gameplay checks and details outside these approvals. The low-camera proposal remains deferred. Slow blocks and special floors are outside frozen art scope; their omission does not authorize removing existing mechanics.
