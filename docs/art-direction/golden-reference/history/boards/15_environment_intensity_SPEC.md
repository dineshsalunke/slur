## Progression and motion

Example rhythm, not a mandated level script: A entry → B build → C peak → A recovery → C climax → B finish. Progression can change over a course and between courses; it need not increase monotonically. Do not suggest physical scenery must visibly inflate or teleport during transitions. Transition behavior remains for later design/implementation review.

Boost is independent: subtle near-field/peripheral motion blur and energetic exhaust are temporary player feedback, not an automatic C setting. No numeric density/exposure/blur budgets are established by this board.

## Review checks

- A/B/C recognizably belong to the same world and retain material identity.
- Gap/obstacle/pickup reads persist at gameplay scale, with bloom on/off and in moving evidence before production acceptance.
- C gains drama from scale/proximity/framing rather than global fog, orange wash or clutter in the route.
- The differences must be visible without reading panel labels.
- Monolith seams remain visible; their glow does not compete with immediate threats.
