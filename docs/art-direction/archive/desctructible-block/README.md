The owner confirmed the collected subject-folder work as final on 2026-09-19. The existing `FINAL_DRAFT` filename is retained for link stability; it no longer indicates pending art approval.

Clean and broadly worn coated-metal variants share sealed rectangular silhouettes, an 8u height envelope and variable strictly vertical marigold seams. Stronger broad wear is permitted. Closed fissures, broken outer contours and exposed energy interiors are excluded from this family. Prefer procedural variation with minimal hand authoring.

The folder spelling was requested by the owner. Its content is **non-destructible blocks**, not the separate broken-contour destructible family. Earlier block proposals are [history](history/README.md).
