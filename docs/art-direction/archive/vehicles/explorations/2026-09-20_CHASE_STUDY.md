# Chase-camera ship study

Status: proposal, not approved. Art direction only; no development changes.

User feedback: the browser game's chase camera makes overall silhouette, rear design, and visible top shapes/lights more valuable than front beauty views.

This study develops Freighter C / Sarcophagus and Comet C / Splinter as the proposed starting pair. It does not freeze either class selection.

- Freighter: long solid hull, raised dorsal spine, broad recessed rear engine bar, paired short top light slots.
- Comet: short broad blade, low central crest, separated twin rear exhausts, paired top wing-root light recesses.
- Preserve dark satin coated metal, faint desaturated slate ambient light, localized marigold energy. Avoid stone-like texture and illuminated outlines.
- Small scene thumbnails are illustrative reduction studies, not verified runtime camera captures or measured scale validation.
