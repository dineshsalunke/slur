The new golden scene is approved. Replacement boards are proposals until individually reviewed; original boards stay archived in place. Immediate deliverable: new environment-intensity board. Remaining families form the next production sequence below.

## Shared board structure

**Track progress — 2026-09-19:** board 24's material/edge direction is accepted and [board 25's procedural wear treatment](track/25_track_procedural_wear_BRIEF.md) is frozen. Measured track sheets, finish-line silhouette and moving readability validation remain outstanding; do not reopen the accepted wear treatment as an unreviewed proposal.

Every family receives a readable visual overview plus detail sheets where necessary, with companion Markdown carrying exact specifications. More detail must not mean illegible tiny text on one crowded image.

Include: purpose and status; silhouette lineup; front/side/top views where meaningful; measured dimensions and dimension order; scale beside a 4u ruler and appropriate ship/block; material assignment and broad surface closeups; finish, reflectivity, bevels/seams and wear; light-source versus emissive versus bloom distinctions; effect/motion states; context at gameplay distance; A/B/C comparisons where relevant; prohibited variants; approval checklist and unresolved points.

Generated numerical labels are not dimensional evidence. Technical geometry/rulers should be constructed and checked deterministically before their sheets claim exact scale. Companion text wins over accidental labels; current game dimensions win over stale companion text. Shader values are explicitly trial presets, not inferred from paintings.

## Acceptance gate

Check each board for frozen silhouette fidelity, dimensional evidence, material consistency, correct light hierarchy, readable labels and separation of approved decisions from proposals. Require actual gameplay-distance/motion review for visibility claims. Do not mark a board approved simply because its source golden scene was approved.
